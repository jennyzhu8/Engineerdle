Engineerdle: the daily engineering puzzle

Each day Engineerdle picks one engineering concept, such as a law, theory, equation, component or phenomenon, from disciplines including thermodynamics, electronics, fluid mechanics, structures, materials, control systems and computer science. You get one clue at a time and five attempts to name it, and each wrong guess or skip reveals a more direct clue.

Once a puzzle's day is over, its answer, explanation and clues are added to the concept library. New here? Read how to play.
